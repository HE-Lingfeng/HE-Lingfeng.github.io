Lab Workflow

From paper intake to research output.

  1. 01

    Ingest

    Bring in PDFs, notes, links, figures, and external literature context.

  2. 02

    Read

    Chunk the paper, synthesize claims, inspect methods, and record limits.

  3. 03

    Archive

    Create literature cards and journals that keep the research memory alive.

  4. 04

    Propose

    Turn gaps into research questions, small experiments, and next actions.

  5. 05

    Present

    Convert the strongest thread into writing, slides, or a project log.
